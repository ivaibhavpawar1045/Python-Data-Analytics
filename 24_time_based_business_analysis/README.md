# Day 24 — Time-Based Business & Delivery Analysis

## 📌 Overview

          Day 24 is part of my Python for Data Analytics learning journey.

          This session focused on **time-based business analysis and operational performance analysis** using the DataCo Supply Chain dataset.

          The objective was to move from transaction-level analysis toward understanding:

          - Sales trends over time
          - Monthly business performance
          - Month-over-Month changes
          - Shipping duration
          - Delivery performance
          - Regional delivery risk
          - Operational risk classification


## 🎯 Objectives

          - Convert date columns into datetime format
          - Extract year and month features
          - Perform monthly sales analysis
          - Compare monthly sales, profit, and orders
          - Perform Month-over-Month analysis
          - Use `shift()` for previous-period comparison
          - Calculate shipping duration
          - Analyze shipping performance by region
          - Compare shipping duration with delivery risk
          - Analyze delivery status
          - Calculate regional late-delivery risk
          - Create operational risk categories
          - Generate business insights from operational data


## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains information about:

          - Orders
          - Customers
          - Products
          - Sales
          - Profit
          - Discounts
          - Customer Segments
          - Regions
          - Delivery Status
          - Shipping Information
