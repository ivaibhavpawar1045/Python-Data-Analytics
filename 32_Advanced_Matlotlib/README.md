          | Category | Total Sales | Contribution |

          | Electronics | ₹22,000 | 25.88% |

## 4. Visualizations
          - Stacked bar chart comparing Online and Store sales
          - Pie chart showing category contribution
          - Box plot displaying order-value distribution
          - Four-chart category performance dashboard

## 5. Business Insights
          - Furniture is the highest-revenue category, contributing 32.94%.
          - Food is the lowest-revenue category.
          - Furniture generated ₹28,000 in total sales.
          - The box plot highlights ₹2,000 as a potential outlier in the sample order values.

          Revenue alone does not establish profitability; cost and margin data would be needed for that assessment.
