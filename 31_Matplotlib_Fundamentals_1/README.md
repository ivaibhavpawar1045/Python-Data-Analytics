# Day 31 — Matplotlib Fundamentals I

## Topics Covered
          - Line charts
          - Vertical and horizontal bar charts
          - Scatter plots
          - Histograms
          - Chart titles, labels, legends and grids
          - Figure sizing
          - Basic KPI calculations
          - Multi-chart dashboards

## Business Dataset
          Seven days of sample sales and customer counts.

## Key Findings
          - Highest daily sales: Sunday — ₹2,500
          - Lowest daily sales: Wednesday — ₹1,100
          - Average daily sales: ₹1,728.57
          - Highest sales per customer: Sunday — ₹62.50

## Visualizations
          1. Daily sales trend
          2. Daily sales comparison
          3. Horizontal sales comparison
          4. Customer count vs. sales scatter plot
          5. Daily sales distribution histogram
          6. Sales per customer bar chart
          7. Two-chart dashboard

## Business Interpretation
          Sunday achieved the highest sales and highest sales per customer in this sample. Wednesday recorded the lowest daily sales.

          The observed association between customer count and sales is descriptive and does not establish causation.
