# 📊 Advanced Groupby & Business Metrics — Day 22

## 📌 Project Overview

          This project focuses on advanced business analysis using Python and Pandas with the DataCo Supply Chain dataset.

## Objective

          The objective was to move beyond basic aggregation and build meaningful business metrics for regional, customer, segment, and product-level analysis.

## 📚 Concepts Covered

### Business Metrics

          - Total Sales
          - Total Profit
          - Average Sales
          - Average Profit
          - Total Orders
          - Profit Margin %
          - Sales per Order
          - Sales Contribution %

### Analytical Operations

          - `sort_values()`
          - `idxmax()`
          - `idxmin()`
          - Boolean filtering
          - Multi-dimensional analysis

## 💼 Key Business Insights

          1. Western Europe is the highest-sales region.

          2. Southern Africa has the highest profit margin, demonstrating stronger profitability efficiency.

          3. Consumer is the dominant customer segment across the analyzed regions.

          4. 227 customers satisfy the defined high-value customer criteria.

          5. Field & Stream Sportsman 16 Gun Fire Safe is the highest-sales product.

          6. Central Africa has the highest late-delivery risk and represents an area for supply-chain investigation.

          7. The region with the highest sales is not necessarily the region with the highest profit margin, demonstrating the importance of using multiple business metrics.

## 🎯 Learning Outcome

          This project strengthened practical Pandas skills for transforming transaction-level data into business-level insights.

          The analysis focused on understanding **why different metrics are needed for business decision-making**, rather than relying only on total sales.
