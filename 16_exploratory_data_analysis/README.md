# Day 16 - Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## 📌 Overview

          - Day 16 focused on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a real-world Superstore sales dataset.

          - The objective was to analyze cleaned sales data, identify important business patterns, and convert numerical results into meaningful business insights.-

## 📊 Dataset

          The cleaned dataset contains:

          - 9,993 records
          - 14 original columns
          - Sales information
          - Customer information
          - Product information
          - Regional information
          - Profit and discount information
          - Date information

          The cleaned dataset was carried forward from Day 15.

## 🎯 Objectives

          - Analyze overall business performance.
          - Compare Sales and Profit.
          - Analyze category performance.
          - Analyze regional performance.
          - Identify loss-making transactions.
          - Analyze losses by category.
          - Identify top customers by total sales.
          - Convert analysis results into business insights.
