# Day 20 - Sales Contribution & Within-Group Analysis

## 📌 Overview

          - Day 20 focused on taking our previous Pandas skills and applying them to more realistic business analysis.

## Objective

          The main objective was to learn how to calculate:

          - Sales by Market
